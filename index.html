What is ESPORA?

A customizable ESP-based IoT platform where anyone can build their own connected world.

ESPORA allows you to take an ESP-based device and turn it into a connected product or experiment that can be controlled through a customized interface. We believe the software should adapt to your project, not the other way around. Whether you need a simple two-button controller for a fan and light, or a massive dashboard for environmental monitoring and robotics—ESPORA scales to your imagination.

Why ESPORA?

The emergence of ESP has revolutionized the way people look at embedded computing systems. Things which were difficult, costly and complicated in the past could be accomplished now with simple means using a cheap ESP module and a little idea. It is because of the connectivity and ease with which ESP can be used. ESPORA is based on this philosophy and aims at adding value to the ESP concept. It aims at developing smart home systems with increased flexibility and adaptability according to one's needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ESPORA?

ESPORA is a highly customizable IoT platform dedicated exclusively to ESP-based microcontrollers, giving you the tools to build, automate, and monitor your connected projects.

Why is ESPORA focused only on ESP?

By focusing solely on the ESP ecosystem, we ensure maximum reliability, deep hardware integration, and a frictionless setup process without the bloat of generic platforms.

Which ESP boards are supported?

We currently support popular ESP-family development boards featuring standard Wi-Fi and BLE capabilities. We are continuously expanding our official support list for newer chipsets.

Can beginners or students use ESPORA?

Absolutely. ESPORA is explicitly designed to be accessible. Students and beginners can start with a simple LED project and immediately see results without a steep learning curve.

Do I need programming knowledge?

No advanced programming degree is required to get started. You can build functional dashboards and basic automations out of the box, while advanced coding tools remain available for power users.

Can I create my own dashboard?

Yes. ESPORA is completely modular. You choose exactly which widgets you need and where they go. Your devices, your layout.

Can I control devices from a phone?

Yes, the ESPORA mobile experience allows you to interact with and control your ESP-based devices seamlessly from your smartphone.

What kind of projects can I build?

ESPORA is incredibly versatile. You can build custom smart home automations, design classroom educational experiments, create robotics interfaces, and monitor environmental sensors.
